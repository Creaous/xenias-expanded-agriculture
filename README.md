# Xenia's Expanded Agriculture

Welcome to Xenia's Expanded Agriculture (also known as XEA)! Enjoy an expanded vanilla game with a focus on weather, seasons, farming, animals, and other aspects to make the game a little bit different.

**NOTE:** There are currently no quests in the game. This modpack is just more focused on discovering things yourself. Good luck!

## Notice

This modpack is still under heavy development. The only official modpack locations are [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/project/xea) and [GitHub](https://github.com/Creaous/xenias-expanded-agriculture).

## Main Notable Mods

With over 200 mods, the experience is vast. These are the core mods that define the survival, farming, and exploration mechanics of the pack.

- **Tough As Nails & Thin Air:** Survival is now a priority. Manage your body temperature, stay hydrated, and carry a respirator before venturing into oxygen-depleted depths.
- **Weather2 & Serene Seasons:** Dynamic weather systems bring hail and tornadoes, while rotating seasons affect crop growth and the environment.
- **The "Let's Do" Series:** A massive collection (Vinery, Bakery, Brewery, Meadow, etc.) that overhauls food production, fermentation, and rural aesthetics.
- **Croptopia & Farmer’s Delight:** Adds hundreds of new crops and a refined cooking system to turn your harvest into high-tier meals.
- **Alex's Mobs & Alex's Caves:** Populates your world with high-quality creatures and unveils massive, bioluminescent, and prehistoric underground biomes.
- **Dynamic Trees:** Trees now grow realistically and fall over when chopped, making forestry feel significantly more immersive.
- **Aquaculture 2:** Overhauls fishing with new rods, loot, and over 30 new fish species to catch in varied biomes.
- **Realistic Horse Genetics:** A deep dive into equine breeding—horses now feature unique coats, stats, and scientifically accurate genetic traits.
- **Domestication Innovation:** Gives your pets a major upgrade with new tricks, talents, and protective gear.

## Technical & World Expansion

The pack isn't just about farming; it features deep automation, space travel, and complete structural overhauls.

- **Create:** Build complex kinetic machinery, automated factories, and sprawling train networks using rotation-based power.
- **Mekanism & Applied Energistics 2:** Advanced tech for the late game. Use Mekanism for high-tier ore processing and AE2 for digitized storage and auto-crafting.
- **Ad Astra:** Build rockets to leave Earth and establish colonies across the Solar System, from the Moon to Mars.
- **The YUNG’s Suite:** Completely replaces vanilla structures like Strongholds, Dungeons, and Mineshafts with massive, complex, and rewarding layouts.
- **Deeper and Darker:** Expands on the Warden’s domain, allowing you to venture through the portal in Ancient Cities into the "Otherside."
- **End Remastered & End's Phantasm:** Reinvents the journey to the End by requiring unique eyes to open the portal and adding lush, vanilla-friendly biomes to the void.
- **Chipped & Macaw’s Series:** Provides thousands of new building variations for blocks, furniture, roofs, and windows to perfect your base.
- **Lootr:** Ensures exploration is fair in multiplayer; every chest is instanced so every player gets their own unique loot.

## Requirements

Please have:

- RAM: Minimum - 4 GB | Recommended - 6-8 GB

### Testing

We have tested the modpack on the following system:

- OS: CachyOS x86_64
- CPU: Ryzen 7 7840HS
- GPU: RTX 4060 Mobile
- RAM: 64 GB (6 GB for Minecraft)
- Display: 2560x1440

FPS was around 300-400 with some dips to around 150-200 in more heavy areas.
